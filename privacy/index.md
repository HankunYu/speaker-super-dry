# Privacy Policy — Speaker Super Dry

_Last updated: October 27, 2026_

Speaker Super Dry does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.
- The app does not connect to the internet.
- The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, or photos.
- Everything the app does happens on your device. It only plays sound through your speaker.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address before the change takes effect.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/HankunYu/speaker-super-dry/issues
