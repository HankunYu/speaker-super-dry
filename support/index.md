# Speaker Super Dry — Support

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at https://github.com/HankunYu/speaker-super-dry/issues

## How to use

- **Tap anywhere** to start the 30-second automatic program. Tap again to stop.
- **Drag left or right** to choose a manual tone between 60 and 500 Hz. It keeps playing after you let go; tap to stop.

## Tips

- Turn the volume all the way up.
- Hold the phone with the speaker facing down so water can drain.
- Disconnect headphones and Bluetooth speakers first; the tone has to play through the built-in speaker.
- Run it two or three times if the sound is still muffled.

## FAQ

**I can't hear anything.** Check the volume, and make sure no headphones or Bluetooth devices are connected.

**Can it fix water damage?** No. It only helps push water out of the speaker grille. If your iPhone was submerged or still sounds wrong, contact Apple or an authorized service provider.

**Does it collect data?** No. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

# 简单的扬声器排水 — 支持

需要帮助或发现问题？请在 https://github.com/HankunYu/speaker-super-dry/issues 提交 issue。

- **轻点屏幕任意位置**开始 30 秒自动程序，再次轻点停止。
- **左右拖动**选择 60–500 Hz 的手动频率，松手后继续播放，轻点停止。
- 建议把音量调到最大，让扬声器朝下，并先断开耳机和蓝牙设备。
- 本 App 不能修复进水造成的损坏；如果 iPhone 曾被浸泡或声音仍不正常，请联系 Apple 或授权服务提供商。

# 簡單的揚聲器排水 — 支援

需要協助或發現問題？請在 https://github.com/HankunYu/speaker-super-dry/issues 提交 issue。

- **輕點螢幕任意位置**開始 30 秒自動程式，再次輕點停止。
- **左右拖動**選擇 60–500 Hz 的手動頻率，放開後繼續播放，輕點停止。
- 建議把音量調到最大，讓揚聲器朝下，並先中斷耳機和藍牙裝置的連線。
- 本 App 不能修復進水造成的損壞；如果 iPhone 曾被浸泡或聲音仍不正常，請聯絡 Apple 或授權服務供應商。
